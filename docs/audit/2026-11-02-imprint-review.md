commercial goods, freelance services or business activity offered. The portrait
may be published. The book PDF/EPUB may remain available as an alpha manuscript;
its download copy now states that it is unfinished and will be revised.

The imprint keeps the confirmed name, place of residence, email, absence of a
business purpose and editorial line. It removes an unconditional claim to
supply information under ECG §5 and the unsupported blanket claim that all
linked content was checked. It does not add a home street address or invent
business registration details. Generic pre-publication advice is removed from
the body; the draft metadata remains pending the owner's choice about external
review and publication.

Sources checked on 2 November:

- [WKO, media-law information for websites](https://www.wko.at/internetrecht/informationspflichten-nach-dem-mediengesetz-fuer-websites): disclosure depends on content, not page count; distinguishes place of residence from full postal address; describes name, residence, business purpose and editorial line, and additional ECG duties where applicable.
- [Austrian business service portal, §25 disclosure](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/brancheninformationen/information-und-kommunikation/offenlegungspflicht-gemaess-para-25-mediengesetz.html): natural-person fields and the editorial line; websites and newsletters have different disclosure/imprint rules.
- [WKO, general imprint guide](https://www.wko.at/internetrecht/website-impressum): ECG duties apply to commercial websites. The owner has confirmed the present non-commercial scope.

The portfolio includes analytical and historical-political essays, so this
review does not rely solely on the narrower personal-presentation exemption;
it retains the editorial line. It assumes the owner is the natural-person
media owner as already declared. It does not certify legal compliance or
claim that WKO/a lawyer reviewed this site. If the site's purpose, ownership,
commercial activity or newsletter offering changes, reassess the obligations.
