# Writing

The Writing index, /en/writing/, and the Writing section of the home page.

- `claim`: the heading, on both. `prose`: the paragraph on the index (the home page has its own shorter line, `writing_prose` in [[Home]]).
- `seo_description`: the index in search results.
- `essays_note`: the line under "All essays" on the home page.
- `contact`: the close of every essay. `{name}` is the name from [[Identity]].
- `feed_description`: the description of the RSS feed.

`&nbsp;` is a no-break space: it keeps the dash on the line of the word before it.
