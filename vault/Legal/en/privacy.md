# Privacy notice

## Controller

Rafael Braga-Kribitz, 8054 Seiersberg, Austria, [hello@rafaelbragakribitz.com](mailto:hello@rafaelbragakribitz.com).

## Hosting and server logs

The website is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When a page is accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary data in server logs: IP address, time, the address requested and the browser identifier. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The storage period and the transfer to the USA are governed by [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); GitHub describes its international-transfer safeguards there, including standard contractual clauses and its Data Privacy Framework self-certification. An independent check of its current registry entry remains pending.

## No cookies, no tracking

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and embeds no fonts, scripts or content from third parties. Every file is loaded from the site’s own origin.

## Local setting

The choice of colour scheme is stored in the browser only after a click (`localStorage`, key `bk-theme`). Only the light or dark theme value is stored. The website does not transmit this setting.

## Contact

Contact emails are processed to answer enquiries and handle follow-up (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Correspondence is retained in Gmail rather than automatically deleted after a reply. Retention is limited to what is needed for that correspondence and any applicable legal obligations.

The email address is provided through an easyname alias or forwarding service; incoming messages are forwarded to the controller’s Gmail inbox. [easyname](https://www.easyname.at/de/unternehmen/privacy-policy) and [Google (Gmail)](https://policies.google.com/privacy) process the message and the data needed for delivery and storage.

## Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR), and a complaint to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, [dsb.gv.at](https://www.dsb.gv.at).
