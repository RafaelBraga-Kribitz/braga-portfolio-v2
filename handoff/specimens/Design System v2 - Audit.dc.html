P-01

Theme model

The page reads its theme from data-theme on the root, falls back to the OS, and defaults to light. A 158-byte pre-paint script applies a stored choice before first paint; the toggle writes storage only after a click. Every dark value lives in one file, and form controls follow the page, not the OS.

P-03

The accent, decided

Owner decision, 20 Oct 2026: the accent may be text. Its two values were chosen for exactly that — 5.3:1 on the light surface, 6.2:1 on dark — so the one figure a view earns is set in the accent, as the site did. The 7px square stays the mark beside a name or a code. What does not change: at most one earned figure per section, siblings in ink-2, and no judgement by colour — a verdict is a word. Gate G-04 now checks that accent text clears 4.5:1 where it sits; on the raised dark surface it does not (3.2:1), so accent text stays on the page surface and the wash. The “never a text colour” sentence is removed from CLAUDE.md so nothing regresses.

01 Work→01-04

Warehouse humanoid TCO

At the modelled demand, humanoids do not yet beat the lean hybrid. A five-year cost model for warehouse pick, move and place work.

OperationsCompletePublished 21 May 2026Updated 16 Sep 2026Repository ↗
Summary

One person with three autonomous mobile robots has the lowest five-year present cost, €453,000, and wins 99.8% of the evaluated draws. Humanoids are deferred until demand, labour supply or shift patterns change.

Key figures
  • €453,000Lowest five-year present cost
  • €0.374Cost per order
  • 99.8%Draws won by the lean hybrid
  • €195,000Capital requirement

Question

At what price, demand and staffing level would a humanoid fleet become the cheaper way to run pick, move and place work in a warehouse? The unit of comparison is five-year present cost at an 8% discount rate.

Data

Capability assumptions use 2,359 episodes from five public UnifoLM datasets, pinned by hash. Household demonstrations stand in for warehouse work, with a speed-transfer factor between 0.50 and 0.90.

Aside

These are not measurements from a deployed warehouse fleet. The proxy is the first limitation listed below.

Model inputs
Episodes
2,359 · five public datasets
Scenarios
7 · 15 replicas each
Throughput
120 orders per hour · utilisation at most 0.85
Uncertainty
10,000 Monte Carlo draws per scenario
Discount rate
8% a year over five years

Method

Four steps, each reproducible from the repository:

  1. Extract capability and timing assumptions from the episodes.
  2. Simulate seven staffing scenarios with discrete events, utilisation capped at 0.85.
  3. Propagate uncertainty through 10,000 draws per scenario.
  4. Run a Sobol sensitivity analysis and a capital-price breakeven search.
Present cost

Future cash flows discounted to today at the stated rate, so that costs falling in different years can be added. Here 8% a year over five years.

def present_cost(cash_flows, rate=0.08):
    """Sum of yearly costs discounted to year 0 (listing for the specimen)."""
    return sum(c / (1 + rate) ** t for t, c in enumerate(cash_flows))

Result

Four of the seven scenario totals were supplied with the brief; the other three are not invented here. The lean hybrid’s capital requirement is €195,000.

Result · 10,000 draws per scenario

The lean hybrid wins 99.8% of the evaluated draws.

Limitations

The ranking holds only under the stated assumptions:

  • Household tasks are an imperfect proxy for warehouse operations.
  • Public capital-cost assumptions are not binding supplier quotes.
  • Collective-agreement labour rates may understate real local costs.1

Decision

Under these assumptions, deploy the lean hybrid and defer humanoids.

What would reverse the decision?

Higher demand, labour scarcity or multiple shifts. Each moves the breakeven capital price; the model is re-run when one of them changes.

P-11

Print

A4 with 16–20mm margins. Paper is the surface, so the light palette is forced on white and pure black ink; hairlines stay; site chrome goes; figures, tables, callouts and rows do not break across pages; no URL is printed after a link. The About/CV page prints as a CV without touching its markup. This page carries the file — print it to see.
