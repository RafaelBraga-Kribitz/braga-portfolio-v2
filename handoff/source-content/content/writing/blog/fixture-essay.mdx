Direct-to-consumer brands live and die by a single question that last-click
attribution quietly gets wrong: did this spend actually cause that revenue? The
platforms that sell the ads also grade their own homework, and the grade is generous.

The argument is narrow. DTC ecommerce does not need more dashboards. It needs
measurement built on incrementality, decisions built on optimization, and a
willingness to attach an honest interval to both.

## The measurement problem

Reported ROAS counts revenue the brand would have earned anyway. Incremental ROAS
counts only what the spend caused, measured against a holdout — a different number,
and usually a smaller one.

```py
def incremental_roas(spend, holdout_lift, baseline):
    """ROAS measured against a holdout, not attribution."""
    incremental_revenue = baseline * holdout_lift
    return incremental_revenue / spend
```

Across the last three campaigns on file, reported ROAS overstated incremental ROAS
by 38% on average — enough to reverse two of six budget decisions.
