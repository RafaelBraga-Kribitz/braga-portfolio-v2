<Lead>
A media-mix model attributing retail/CPG spend to share-of-voice across the DACH region, built
to replace a last-touch heuristic.
</Lead>

A retail/CPG account measured share-of-voice with last-touch attribution — a method
that rewards whichever channel happened to close, not whichever channel actually moved
the number. The brief: replace it with a Bayesian media-mix model that attributes
spend to outcome across the DACH region's channel mix, with an honest interval on
every coefficient.

The model is in build; the figures below are placeholders pending the first full fit
against twelve months of regional spend and share-of-voice data.
