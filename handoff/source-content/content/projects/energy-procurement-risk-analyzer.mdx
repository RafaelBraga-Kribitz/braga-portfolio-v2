<Lead>
What did buying electricity the wrong way cost a Styrian manufacturer using 50 GWh a year in
2021–2025, and what is the P95 cost exposure for the next twelve months under each procurement
strategy? Ingest and warehouse are built; <strong>strategy results do not exist yet</strong>.
</Lead>

After the 2021–2023 European energy crisis, electricity procurement became a board-level risk for
Austrian industry. The project compares four strategies on the Austrian day-ahead market (full
spot, an indexed contract, a fixed annual price and a partial hedge) for one constructed 50 GWh
industrial load.

<Marker num="01" label="Context" id="sec-01" />

The market data is real: Austrian and German-Luxembourg day-ahead prices and Austrian load and
generation from the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, the Austrian Energy Agency's monthly price
index (ÖSPI, transcribed by hand with double entry), daily mean temperature for Graz from
GeoSphere Austria, and Austrian and Styrian public holidays. No market price is synthetic. The
window runs from January 2019 to the latest complete month.

Two inputs are assumptions and are tagged as such. The consumer's load shape is constructed, not
measured, and ÖSPI stands in for contract and forward prices. Grid fees, taxes and levies are out
of scope, so the analysis isolates the energy-price lever.

<Marker num="02" label="Process" id="sec-02" />

Built so far: ingestion from all four sources with retries, caching and validation gates, into
raw Parquet, and a DuckDB warehouse modelled in dbt from staging to marts. The gates exist so a
plausible-looking file cannot pass silently. They check hour coverage including the 23- and
25-hour daylight-saving days, price and load plausibility, the Austria–Germany join and the
presence of negative prices. dbt tests check the mart schema contract, daylight-saving adjacency,
a price reconciliation for August 2022 and gaps in the month spine. CI runs lint, the tests and a
network-free dbt build against a fixture warehouse.

Still to build: the load profile, market analytics, four strategy cost engines with a seeded
block bootstrap for forward risk (P95 and CVaR95), and the reporting layer. No price forecast is
planned: strategies are scored on realised prices and on resampling them.

<Marker num="03" label="Results" id="sec-03" />

None yet. By the project's own rule, every published number must first appear, with its
epistemic tag, in an auto-generated numeric source-of-truth file, and that file arrives with the
strategy simulator. Until then this page quotes no result.
