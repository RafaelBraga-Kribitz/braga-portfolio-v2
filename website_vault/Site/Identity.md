# Identity

Who the site is about, once for every page: the header and footer, the cover, the Impressum line, the browser title, the share card and the data search engines read.

- `name`, `wordmark`: the name as written, and the mark in the header.
- `role_en`, `role_de`: the job title. It is the home page's browser title and the role in the search data. The big title on the cover is `title_lines` in [[Home]].
- `location_en`, `location_de`: "Based in" on the cover and in the footer.
- `github`, `linkedin`, `kaggle`, `tableau`: the footer's links. Leave one empty and its link disappears.
