<Lead>
Rebuilding a national-scale allocation system so spend follows expected outcome rather than
precedent.
</Lead>

The incumbent system allocated budget by historical share — a process that compounded
last year's decisions into this year's baseline. The mandate was to reconstruct the
allocation logic end to end, making every unit of spend traceable to an expected
outcome rather than an inherited line item.

<Marker num="01" label="Context" id="sec-01" />

The program distributes a fixed annual budget across fifty thousand entities,
evaluated over a rolling fourteen-week window. Outcomes are the share of entities
reaching a defined threshold — the outcome margin. The baseline system delivered
42.73%.

<Marker num="02" label="Process" id="sec-02" />

Formally: allocate a fixed budget across segments to maximize expected aggregate
outcome, subject to a binding cap and non-negativity. The objective is linear in
spend once per-segment elasticities are estimated.

<MathBlock display tex="\max_{x}\; \sum_{i=1}^{8} w_i\, x_i \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \sum_{i=1}^{8} x_i = B,\; x_i \ge 0" />

Elasticities are estimated with a partially-pooled Bayesian model; the allocator that
consumes them is a MILP solved under the cap:

```py
from allocator import solve_milp

def reconstruct(weights, budget=1_250_000.0):
    """Return the optimal allocation under a binding cap."""
    plan = solve_milp(weights, cap=budget, floor=0.0)
    assert abs(plan.spend.sum() - budget) < 1e-6  # cap is binding
    return plan
```

<Callout variant="note">
  Roughly a third of the prior allocation logic existed only as undocumented
  convention. Making it explicit was the precondition for any optimization to be
  trustworthy.
</Callout>

<Marker num="03" label="Results" id="sec-03" />

Deployed across the full fifty-thousand-entity population, the reconstructed system
lifted the outcome margin from 42.73% to 46.43% — a +3.70 pp improvement on the metric
that matters, achieved within the same fixed budget.

At this scale, +3.70 pp represents roughly eighteen hundred additional entities
crossing the threshold each window. Because the cap is binding, the gain came from
placement, not spend.
