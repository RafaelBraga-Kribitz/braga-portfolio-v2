Reviewed the current uncommitted DE/EN privacy and accessibility notices against `docs/audit/2026-11-01-privacy-sources.md` and the confirmed owner facts. Read-only product review; no builds or legal approval. All four notices retain `translationStatus: draft`.

**Result: semantic parity passes; no blocking German-language defect found.**

- **Privacy:** DE and EN consistently describe easyname forwarding to Gmail and purpose-limited retention rather than deletion immediately after a reply. Neither invents a Google contracting entity, account type, storage country, contract or fixed retention period. Provider links match the verified official URLs. GitHub SCC/DPF statements are attributed to GitHub; independent registry verification remains expressly pending, matching the source review. The theme-storage wording now describes the stored value and site transmission behavior without making an absolute personal-data classification.
- **Retention qualification:** The owner has received no enquiries yet and normally would retain correspondence. The new purpose limit describes the intended practice; it is not evidence of an established deletion/review process. Both notices appropriately retain a pending review of contact-retention practice. Do not mark that operational or legal check complete from this language pass.
- **Accessibility:** Both versions remove the unsupported universal keyboard/reading-order guarantee, completed manual/zoom checks, promised NVDA pass and categorical BaFG exemption. They disclose incomplete manual coverage, desktop focus-order divergence and the hidden-email limitation without JavaScript. The introductory WCAG sentence expresses an aim, not achieved conformance; the limitations explicitly say automated checks do not establish full conformance. Automated test scope is equivalent in both languages; its execution is outside this language review.
- **German:** Terminology, grammatical cases, meaning and numerical formatting are acceptable. The shortcut sentence ending “wirken sie nicht, statt diese modifizierten Befehle zu ersetzen” is comprehensible but unnecessarily literal. Optional smoother replacement: “Beim Tippen in ein Feld oder mit gehaltener Cmd-, Strg- oder Alt-Taste sind die Kürzel deaktiviert. Diese modifizierten Befehle werden nicht ersetzt.” No meaning change is needed.

The source review verifies generic provider-policy statements and working links, not account-specific contract terms, actual mail retention or legal sufficiency. Those distinctions remain intact. No product files or approval flags were changed.
